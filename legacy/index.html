Waste Segregation — Why It Matters

Waste that isn't segregated correctly at home cannot be fixed later. Even a small amount of food-soiled dry waste contaminates the entire load, forcing it to be sent to landfill.

These landfills are already overflowing, and one lies within 10 km of our homes. Large volumes of waste are routinely burnt — meaning we end up breathing the smoke.

Correct segregation at home is one of the few actions that truly prevents this.
